## The question

Can an electrochemically controlled local environment provide a selective route to separating rare-earth elements?

## Research direction

I am exploring electrochemical approaches to rare-earth separation, with an emphasis on spatial and temporal control near electrode interfaces. The broad goal is to understand how interfacial reactions and transport can be coordinated to influence selectivity.

This direction brings together three themes from my broader research: **interfacial chemistry**, **reaction kinetics**, and **mass transport**. It extends my work from storing and converting energy toward electrochemically controlled resource recovery.
